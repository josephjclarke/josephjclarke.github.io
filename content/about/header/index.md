I am a Senior Scientist in the Met Office Hadley Centre. I research nonlinear behaviour in the climate-carbon system. For example,
I am interested in topics broadly related to tipping points, such as
overshoot, irreversibility, feedbacks and sensitivity. I'm particularly interested in non-autonomous tipping phenomena such as rate induced tipping.
As well as using models, I am interested in
empirical approaches to measuring changes in the stability of potential tipping elements using
early warning signals. In addition to my background in climate science, I use techniques from
dynamical systems, statistical and stochastic dynamics to understand these nonlinear phenomena
in the Earth system. You may be interested in my [CV](/cv.pdf).
